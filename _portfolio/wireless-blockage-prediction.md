**November 2023 – December 2023**

Combined convolutional and recurrent layers into a single neural network architecture to predict future blockage events capable of interrupting wireless communication between fixed transmitters and receivers. Testing on the DeepSense 6G dataset demonstrated that the mixed architecture could outperform pure CNN or RNN architectures for certain prediction horizons.
