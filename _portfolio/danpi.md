**May 2022 – June 2022**

Improved upon an existing audio normalization algorithm by replacing several of its algorithms with parallel algorithms which can process large amounts of audio samples more efficiently. Performance tests indicated that the normalization process is accelerated by up to a factor of 12 when processing 10 million audio samples.
