**April 2024 – May 2024**

Analyzed why the Silver stepsize schedule improves convergence rates for unconstrained gradient descent on smooth convex functions. Empirically studied whether the benefits of the Silver stepsize schedule extend to constrained optimization, and found that in certain cases the benefit can only be realized by adapting the schedule when projection occurs.
